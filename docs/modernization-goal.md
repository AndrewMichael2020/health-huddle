# Modernization goal

Skagit Health Authority's fictional LTC portfolio needs a dependable data
foundation for foreseeable authorized analytical and operational workloads.
The immediate goal is not to move the greatest number of tables. It is to prove
that one cross-system LTC slice can be ingested repeatedly, reconciled to its
sources, traced through transformations, and released under explicit controls.

The foundation must support Power BI semantic models, operational monitoring,
data-quality investigation, portfolio planning, and future forecasting without
placing report-specific logic in source ingestion.

## Principles

1. Preserve authorized source meaning and provenance.
2. Standardize only where commonality is legitimate.
3. Make rejected and uncertain records visible.
4. Treat reconciliation as a design requirement, not a final comparison.
5. Let service objectives determine readiness and architecture decisions.
